Common questions

The voice sounds robotic

Your iPhone has a nicer Indonesian voice you can download for free:

  1. Open Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices.
  2. Tap Indonesian, then pick a voice marked Enhanced or Premium and download it.
  3. Reopen Learn Bahasa. It uses the best Indonesian voice on your phone.
Speaking practice doesn't hear me

Speaking uses Apple's on-device Indonesian dictation, so nothing leaves your phone. Make sure Learn Bahasa is allowed to use the microphone and speech recognition (Settings › Learn Bahasa), and add Indonesian under Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards so iOS downloads its dictation. On older iPhones on-device Indonesian may not be available. You can always tap your reply instead.

Restore my purchase

Open the Bahasa Plus screen in the app and tap Restore. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. To manage or cancel a subscription, go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Reset my progress

Progress lives only on your iPhone. To start fresh, delete Learn Bahasa and install it again. Your subscription comes back when you tap Restore.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your progress comes along when you restore the new phone from an iCloud or computer backup. There's no account, so there's nothing to sign in to.

Is the Indonesian natural?

Yes, that's the whole point. Every line is everyday Indonesian as people speak it in Bali and Java, casual forms included, and it's checked by native speakers. Spotted something odd? Tell us and we'll fix it.

Does Learn Bahasa collect my data?

No. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. See the privacy policy.
