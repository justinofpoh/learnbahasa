Privacy policy

Short version: without an account, your progress stays on your device. If you choose to sign in, we store your progress so it syncs to your other devices, and nothing else.

What we collect

Nothing personal unless you choose to sign in. Learn Bahasa has no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and works fully without an account. If you sign in, we keep only what's needed to sync your progress (below). Subscription status is described under Purchases.

Your progress

Lessons done, chats played, your streak, your name and country (if you add them) and your saved lines are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, Apple may include this data in that backup under Apple's own terms.

If you sign in (optional)

You can sign in with Apple or Google to keep your progress in sync between the app and learnbahasa.app. Then we store, in our database at Supabase: your email address (with Sign in with Apple you can hide it behind an Apple relay address), an account ID, and your progress (lessons and chats finished, XP, your name, country, chosen avatar and level, and saved lines). We use it only to sync your progress. We never sell it or use it for ads. Your subscription is linked to the same account ID at RevenueCat so Plus follows you.

You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile › Delete account) or on the website. That removes the account and everything stored with it straight away.

Microphone and speech recognition

Speaking practice is optional. If you turn it on, iOS asks for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition. Learn Bahasa uses Apple's on-device recognition only: your voice is turned into text on your iPhone and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. You can switch these permissions off any time in Settings › Privacy & Security.

Voices

The Indonesian you hear is read aloud by Apple's built-in text-to-speech on your device. No audio is sent over the internet.

Purchases

If you subscribe or buy something in the app, Apple handles the payment through the App Store. Learn Bahasa never sees your name, card or payment details. It only learns whether your purchase is active, so it can unlock the content. Subscriptions are handled through RevenueCat, a service that checks your App Store receipts: it receives your purchase details (which plan, when it renews) and a random ID created for this app, never your name, email or card. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

The website

The free lessons and chats on learnbahasa.app save your progress, name and country in your own browser (local storage) only. Nothing is sent to us, and there are no cookies, ads or analytics. Our web host keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run the site securely. Lines are played from recordings made with a computer-generated voice (Supertonic by Supertone), or read by your browser's built-in voice.

Children

Learn Bahasa doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If we ever wanted to collect anything, we'd say so here first.
