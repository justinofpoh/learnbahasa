Description:
Learn the Indonesian you'll actually use in Bali.

Learn Bahasa teaches how Indonesian sentences work, then lets you practise them in real conversations: at the warung, on the back of an ojek, at the market, the laundry, the pharmacy and the temple.

TALK TO YOUR NEIGHBOURS
Twelve illustrated chats with people you'll really meet. Ibu Wayan asks what you'd like to eat. Pak Agus wants to know where you're from. Bu Ketut starts the sarong at 200 ribu. Listen first, pick your replies, then say them yourself.

UNDERSTAND THE SHAPE OF A SENTENCE
Eleven short stages, from "Selamat pagi" to linking your ideas. No verb tables: Indonesian has no tenses, no plurals to memorise and no "to be". Colour-coded patterns show you why a sentence works.

TAP ANY WORD
Every Indonesian word in a chat is a link. Tap it for its meaning, its role and a tip from Kak Dewi.

READY FOR THE REAL THING
A cheat card for each place, a phrasebook you can search, natural voices for every line, and practice that brings back the lines you missed.

A FEW MINUTES A DAY
Keep a streak, set a gentle daily reminder and add the widget to see who's waiting for you.

Free: the warung and ojek chats, the first two stages, every pattern and the whole phrasebook.
