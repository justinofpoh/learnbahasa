Description:
Learn Bahasa teaches Indonesian the way you will actually use it: how a sentence is put together, then the conversations that come up when you travel or live here.

LESSONS
Eleven short stages on the patterns English speakers trip over. Verbs never change. There is no word for "is" before an adjective. Describers follow the noun. Time words do the work that tenses do in English. After each idea you pick the right line, build it from tiles, or listen and choose. A miss comes back once before the lesson ends, with the correct answer and why.

TALK
Twelve conversations with neighbours you will really meet. Order nasi campur at a warung, ride with an ojek driver, haggle for a sarong, rent a kos, drop off your laundry, ask at the apotek, fix a flat tyre, rent a scooter, order at a cafe, visit a temple, and get invited for coffee. Listen first, then pick your replies, then say them yourself. A wrong reply shows a hint. A right one becomes your line, and the chat moves on.

TAP ANY WORD
Every Indonesian word in a chat is a link. Tap it for its meaning, the job it does in the sentence, and a tip. Every line has a natural voice, and a slow button when you need it.

PATTERNS AND PHRASES
Every grammar idea from the lessons, in one list, coloured by the job each word does. A phrasebook for the lines you need on the spot: food, directions, shopping, home, travel, culture, and what to say when something goes wrong. Tap any line to hear it.

A FEW MINUTES A DAY
Keep a streak, turn on a gentle daily reminder, and add the widget to see who is waiting for you. Lines you missed come back on later days until they stick.
