APP REVIEW NOTES · Learn Bahasa 1.0

1. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Learn Bahasa teaches everyday Indonesian to English speakers travelling to or living in Indonesia, especially Bali. Short lessons explain how Indonesian sentences work (word order, time words instead of tenses, negation, questions, prefixes). Then learners rehearse real conversations with illustrated neighbours: ordering at a warung, an ojek ride, haggling at the market, renting a room, laundry, the pharmacy, the motorbike workshop, a cafe, renting a scooter, visiting a temple and chatting with a neighbour.

2. SETUP AND ACCESS
No account or login is needed. Sign-in is optional and only syncs progress with the website (learnbahasa.app/learn).
First launch: a short welcome (7 pages) asks for a first name, country and a quick level check, then opens the first chat. To skip it, tap through with Next.
- Learn tab: 11 units, 38 lessons. Tap "Up next" or any lesson row.
- Talk tab: 12 chats ("Scenes"). Open one and tap Start. A chat runs in steps: listen first, then choose replies, then type or say them. Tap any Indonesian word to see its meaning.
- Patterns tab: grammar reference. Phrases tab: phrasebook with search; tap to hear.
- Profile button (top right of every tab): avatar, Bahasa Plus, account, level, chat speed, daily reminder.
- Home-screen and Lock Screen widget "Daily Bahasa": streak, today's goal and the next neighbour.

3. IN-APP PURCHASES (Bahasa Plus)
Auto-renewable subscriptions, group "Bahasa Plus":
- com.justinpoh.bahasa.plus.annual: yearly, 7-day free trial
- com.justinpoh.bahasa.plus.monthly: monthly
Free forever: the warung and ojek chats, Learn units 1-2, all Patterns, all Phrases and word lookup.
Where the paywall appears:
- Talk tab: tap any chat card marked "Plus" (for example Bu Ketut at the pasar).
- Learn tab: any lesson from unit 3 on.
- After the learner finishes their first chat (one time).
- Profile button > Bahasa Plus.
The paywall shows price, period, trial terms, Restore Purchases, Terms of Use (Apple standard EULA) and the Privacy Policy. Purchases are processed by Apple StoreKit through the RevenueCat SDK. Manage or cancel: Profile > Bahasa Plus > Manage subscription. A local notification reminds trial users the day before the trial ends (only if they allowed notifications).

4. ACCOUNTS (optional)
Profile > Account > Sign in with Apple, or Continue with Google. This only saves and syncs learning progress (lessons, XP, chats, first name, country) with learnbahasa.app. Account deletion is in the app: Profile > Account > Delete account (deletes the account and its saved progress immediately).

5. PERMISSIONS
- Microphone and Speech Recognition: only when the learner taps the microphone to say a reply. Recognition is on-device only; typing always works instead.
- Notifications: asked only when the learner turns on the daily reminder (a card on the Learn tab after their first lesson or chat, or Profile > Daily reminder).

6. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- RevenueCat: subscription status (purchase history, not linked to identity unless signed in).
- Supabase: optional account and progress sync (email, user ID, name, progress).
No ads, no tracking, no analytics SDKs.
Voices: Indonesian audio is pre-recorded with Supertonic (computer-generated, licence allows commercial use) and bundled in the app; lines without a recording use Apple's on-device id-ID voice.

7. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None.

8. CONTENT
All lessons, conversations, phrases, illustrations and the icon are original work by the developer.
