2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Bahasa teaches Indonesian to English speakers who live in or travel to Indonesia (expats, digital nomads, long-stay visitors, especially in Bali). Most language apps teach isolated vocabulary. Bahasa explains how Indonesian sentences are built (word order, time markers instead of tenses, negation, questions, prefixes) and then has learners practise real everyday conversations: ordering at a warung, haggling at a market, riding a Gojek, renting a room and making small talk. The app gives learners the grammar and the confidence to hold simple real conversations from the first week.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS TO MAIN FEATURES
No account, login, sign-up or sample files are needed. All content is free and unlocked on first launch. There is no user-generated content and no in-app purchases.
- Learn tab: 7 units / 15 lessons. Tap "Up next" or any lesson row to open a lesson. Steps include concept cards, multiple choice, sentence building with word tiles and listening exercises. Tap Check to grade each step. Progress, XP and streak are saved on the device.
- Talk tab: 5 Scenes (warung, ojek ride, market, renting a room, coffee with a friend), each with an animated illustration. Open a Scene and work through its stages in order: Watch (listen to the conversation, then answer 3 questions), Rehearse (choose or build replies), Perform (type or speak replies), Live (a cheat card and a check-in after trying it for real) and Recall (missed lines come back on later days). Every stage is free; later stages unlock after the earlier ones in the same Scene.
- Speaking in Perform is optional. Tapping the microphone asks for Microphone and Speech Recognition permission. Recognition runs on-device only (requiresOnDeviceRecognition); if the device has no on-device Indonesian model, the app says so and typing works instead.
- Patterns tab: grammar reference built from the lessons. Tap any row for details.
- Phrases tab: phrasebook of 58 everyday phrases in 7 categories, with search. Tap a phrase to hear it spoken.
Speaker icons read Indonesian aloud using the device's built-in Indonesian (id-ID) voice. The tortoise button plays it slowly. If audio is silent, make sure the ringer is on and the Indonesian voice is available in Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app runs fully offline and makes no network requests. It uses no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising, authentication, payment or AI services. All lesson content is bundled in the app. Speech output uses Apple's on-device AVSpeechSynthesizer, and optional speech input uses on-device SFSpeechRecognizer only, so no audio leaves the device. Progress is stored locally in UserDefaults and is never transmitted.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same and shows the same content in every region where it is available. The app interface is in English and the content teaches standard everyday Indonesian.
